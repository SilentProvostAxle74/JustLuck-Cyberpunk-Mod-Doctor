## Suggested first release
`v0.1.0-ui-preview`

**Release title:** `JustLuck Mod Doctor v0.1.0 — Interactive UI Preview`

**Release description:**

An interactive look at the proposed JustLuck Cyberpunk Mod Doctor experience. Explore the mod library, sample diagnostic views, and planned backup workflow in your browser. This early concept is intended to collect community feedback before Windows integration and live mod scanning are implemented.

**How to preview:** Download the source ZIP and open `app/index.html` in your browser.

**Important:** Do not label the release as a functioning scanner, optimizer, backup tool, or installer. The sample information is not derived from the viewer's game.

## Suggested community post

**Title:** `Concept: Cyberpunk 2077 Mod Doctor — would you use a tool for dependency checks and mod troubleshooting?`

**Body:**

I'm exploring a Windows companion for Cyberpunk 2077 modding, focused on dependency checks, understandable diagnostics, and safer backups. I made a navigable UI prototype so people can see the proposed workflow before I build real filesystem integration.

What issues take the most time when you troubleshoot a modded setup? What would a new tool need to do better than your current workflow?

Screenshots and a browser demo are in the repo. I'd appreciate candid feedback.
